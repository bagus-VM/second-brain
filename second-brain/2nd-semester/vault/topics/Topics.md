# Topics — Semester 2

Chapter-level overviews: one page per lecture (or exercise unit), tying that session's concepts
together and pointing at the pages it produced.

## Naming convention

`<course-slug>-lecture-N.md`, `…-exercise-N.md` — slugs only, no spaces. The course slug is the same
one used in `courses/<slug>.md`, so both layers sort together.

## What each lecture page covers

- A one-line summary of what the session was actually about
- A **core map**: how this lecture's concepts hang together
- **Key concepts at a glance**: every page the lecture produced, with its one-line idea
- **Lecture structure**: the slide-level outline
- **Exam-relevant points**: what is testable here (and where the lecture left it open)
- **Connections**: prerequisite lectures, homework, and other courses
- **Open questions**: what the source does not settle
