## Dropped courses

**Hardware-Oriented Security** (Katzenbeisser) and **Reverse- and Reengineering** (Hammer) were
dropped by the student on 2026-10-20. Their course pages, 24 concept pages, 4 lecture pages,
2 homework solutions and all raw lecture PDFs were removed from the vault.

**Reverse- and Reengineering (Hammer) was re-registered on 2026-10-22.** The student took the
course back up and the material was re-ingested: 2 lecture decks, 2 homework sheets, 10 concept
pages, 4 topic pages and 2 homework solutions are back in the vault. It is an **active course**
again as of 2026-10-22 and is in scope for exam prep. The 2026-10-20 drop above is kept as the
historical record, not as the current state. Two further lecture/sheet pairs followed: lecture 3
with homework 3 (2026-10-29), lecture 4 with homework 4 (2026-11-05), and lecture 5 with homework 5 (2026-11-12).

**Energy Informatics 1** (de Meer) was dropped by the student on 2026-11-07, replaced in the timetable by Search-Based Software Engineering (Fraser, Feldmeier). Its course page, 6 concept pages (power-grid fundamentals, alternating-current basics, demand-side integration, sector coupling, the ICT energy footprint, the Paris Agreement climate goals), its single lecture page and the tutorial-1 page were removed, together with the four raw PDFs (which were git-tracked, so the removal is recoverable with `git checkout`). Nothing from it is in scope for exam prep.

**Hardware-Oriented Security** remains dropped. Nothing from it is in scope for exam prep.

**Scaling Database Systems** (Scherzinger) was dropped by the student on 2026-10-21. Its course
page, 3 concept pages, 1 topic page and all 4 raw PDFs were removed. The course was the only one
in the semester with a fully mandatory logistics chain: group sign-up (capped, closed
20 Oct 23:59), graded milestones submitted only through Praktomat with hard cutoffs, and a
Docker server application deadline of 19 Oct. Nothing from it is in scope for exam prep.
