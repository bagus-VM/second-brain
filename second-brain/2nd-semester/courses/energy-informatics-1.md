## One-line Summary
How ICT is used to make the electrical grid smart: the physics and economics of the grid, then the architectures, standards, and protocols that manage it.

## Positioning

Complementary to *Foundation of Energy Systems* (now *Basic Principles of Power System Operation*): FoES explains the electrical grid's physics; this course explains how to make that grid **smart** using ICT. Emphasis on computer networks, telecommunication technologies, and smart-grid modelling.

## Tutorial 1 (discussion 20 and 22 October 2026)

Three tasks, all answerable from the motivation lecture:

1. **Alternating current** — model AC as a function of time; amplitude, period, frequency; RMS value; peak vs. RMS for German residential outlets (230 V RMS → ≈325 V peak).
2. **Sector coupling** — definition, advantages, disadvantages; real vs. virtual energy conversion with an example.
3. **Grid assumptions** — can frequency indicate the demand/supply balance? State the traditional assumptions; what do renewables break?
