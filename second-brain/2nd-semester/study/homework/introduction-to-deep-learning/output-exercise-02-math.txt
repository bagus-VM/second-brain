3(d) p_X(x) = 3x^2 - e^x on [2,4]
  values: ['4.6109', '5.6998', '6.5675', '7.0449', '6.9145', '5.8972', '3.6345', '-0.3336', '-6.5982']
  integral from 2 to 4 = 8.790908   (must be 1)
  p(4) = -6.598148  (must be >= 0)
  sign change at x* = 3.733079  -> p < 0 on (x*, 4]
  => NOT a valid PDF: negative on part of the support, and the integral
     is not 1 either.  Fix: restrict to a subinterval where p >= 0 and
     renormalise: p~ = p / integral over that subinterval.

  fix: support [2, 3.733079]
  mass on that support = 9.605157
  renormalised density p~/ = p(x)/9.605157
  check: p~(2) = 0.480049
  check: p~(x*) = 0.000000
  check: integral of p~ over [2, x*] = 1.000000
  check: p~(2) >= 0: True
