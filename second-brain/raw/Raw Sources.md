# Raw Sources — IMMUTABLE

This folder contains your source material. Professor White **reads** from here — never **writes** here.

## Where to put what

| Subfolder | What goes here |
|-----------|---------------|
| `raw/lectures/` | Lecture slides, scanned notes, professor PDFs |
| `raw/papers/` | Assigned readings, research papers |
| `raw/textbooks/` | Textbook chapters, reference material |
| `raw/assets/` | Images, diagrams, screenshots referenced by other sources |

## How to use

1. Drop your files into the right subfolder
2. Tell Professor White: `ingest [filename]`
3. Professor White will read, synthesize, and write compiled pages into `vault/`

**Do not edit files here after ingest.** Raw sources are the source of truth. If a lecture gets updated, add the new version as a separate file.
