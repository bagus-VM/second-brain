# Connections — Vault

Cross-topic insight pages. These are where the real understanding lives:

- Analogies between seemingly different concepts
- Contradictions or tensions between approaches
- Surprising relationships discovered during study
- Deep explanations that emerged from QUERY sessions

## Naming convention
Use the two concepts connected: `dynamic-programming-vs-greedy.md`

## Why these matter
Good answers don't disappear into chat history. They compound into the vault. Connections pages are where understanding deepens — not just "what is X" but "how does X relate to Y, and why does that matter?"

Professor White creates these during INGEST and QUERY operations.
