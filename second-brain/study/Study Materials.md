# Study Materials — Active Exam Preparation

Professor White generates and maintains these on demand. This is where vault knowledge becomes exam-ready.

## Structure

| Subfolder | What lives here |
|-----------|----------------|
| `study/flashcards/` | Spaced-repetition Q&A cards per topic |
| `study/practice/` | Problem sets, coding challenges, proof exercises |
| `study/exams/` | Past papers, mock exams, Professor White exam drafts |

## How to use

- **DRILL** (`drill me on [topic]`): Professor White generates questions on the spot
- **PREP** (`exam prep for [course]`): Full exam battle plan with mock exam
- Flashcards and practice problems are generated from vault content — they stay current as the vault grows
