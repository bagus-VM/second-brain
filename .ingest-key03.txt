ia64 ABI*
Differs from previous Intel processors (16/32 bit)
RAX, RCX, RDX, R8, R9, R10, R11, and XMM0-XMM5 are considered volatile (potentially changed by callee on return)
RBX, RBP, RDI, RSI, RSP, R12, R13, R14, R15, and XMM6-XMM15 nonvolatile: must be saved and restored by a function using them
Integer arguments passed in registers RCX, RDX, R8, and R9, space must be reserved on stack for callee to spill them, further parameters are passed on the stack in right-to-left order
Floating point arguments are passed in XMM0L, XMM1L, XMM2L, and XMM3L
Return value stored in RAX or XMM0
